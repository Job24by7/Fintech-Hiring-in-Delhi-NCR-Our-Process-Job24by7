## The Hiring Gaps Fintech Teams Feel Every Quarter

Talent shortage comes first. Plenty of candidates list payments or lending on their resumes, but far fewer have handled reconciliation failures, regulator queries, or fraud spikes in production. Demand from Gurugram, Noida and Delhi has stretched that small pool thin.

High employee turnover follows closely. A hire made under pressure often misjudges the stack, the pace or the growth path, and the person is gone within a few months. Each exit restarts the search.

Unverified candidates are the quieter danger. Inflated titles, borrowed project credit, and unexplained career gaps slip through rushed interviews, and in a business that handles other people’s money, that is a risk rather than an inconvenience.

Then there is the lengthy hiring process. Strong candidates rarely wait through four rounds over three weeks when a faster offer is on the table. Every vacant seat, every rehire, and every delayed product release pushes operational costs higher, and finance teams feel it in the next budget review.
